## MFO Classes Add-on v1.0.1 (alpha)

The class add-on is its own download. It adds 35 classes that a follower earns by playing, on a tree with four tiers. This is an alpha. Tiers 1 and 2 are tuned by the simulator. Tiers 3 and 4 are built but not tuned.

**Needs**
- MFO v2.11.1, with the v2.11.1 tuning update files (`MFO-v2.11.1-tuning-update.zip` from the MFO v2.11.1 release)
- Harbinger v0.20.1 (ABI v45). Without it the add-on stays off and says so once in the log and once in game.

**What it does**
- 35 classes in four tiers, with a Classes tab on the board, packages, XP, an MCM page, default gambits, dodge rates and school affinity
- The 15 class cells (three tree-off bases plus the tier 1 and 2 classes) use the final tuning round values from the v2.11.1 tuning files
- Class data is saved in the MCLS v2 record

**Install**
1. Install this zip as its own mod, after MFO.
2. Enable `MFO_Classes.esl` in your load order.
3. Make sure MFO v2.11.1 and Harbinger v0.20.1 are installed.

**Uninstall**
Disable or remove `MFO_Classes.esl`. MFO stays on and the class tree goes inert. Class data already in your saves is kept and written back unchanged, so you can enable the add-on again later.

**Known issues**
- Alpha. Not field tested beyond the first sessions. Keep a backup of your saves.
- Tiers 3 and 4 are untuned, so their numbers are placeholders.
- Living Legend does not yet dodge only when his block does not.

This add-on is not on Nexus.
