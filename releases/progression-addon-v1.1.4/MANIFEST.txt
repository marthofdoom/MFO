v1.1.4 (changes since v1.1.2)
- Followers pick their perks properly again. MFO decides which perks actually do something on a follower (some only ever work for the player). Since MFO 1.1.3 that list lives in the add-on instead of inside MFO, and the old 1.1.2 add-on did not carry it. With MFO 1.1.3 or newer and the 1.1.2 add-on, every perk of that kind was marked as doubtful and ranked lower. This version carries the full list again.
- Ranged followers train Light Armor only. A Ranged follower whose starting stats leaned toward Heavy Armor (Adelinda, for one) trained Heavy Armor. Ranged is strictly Light Armor now.
