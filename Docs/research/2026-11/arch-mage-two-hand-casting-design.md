Status: design only. Raised by the 2026-11-17 Deck run (FINDINGS "Arch-Mage sighting"); ruling by marth, verbatim:
"they dont need to be fired left and right at the same time always, arhcmage unique will treat left and right as independent casting sources, and know whats on the other one at all times. So it may fire at the same time, or it may be a stride increasing concentration in one hand with an attack or ward in the other. Regular casters, may do this, but significantly less intelligently."

## The mechanism to build
Two casting SOURCES, L and R, each a small state machine owned by one hand:
`Idle -> Claimed(spell, target, kind) -> Charging -> Ready -> Firing -> Cooldown`, read from `CastInFlightOnHand` / `CastChargedWaitingOnHand` (cast/Hands.cpp) plus the claim record (`APMFBridge::GetHealCastSpell/Target` per hand, the offense claim, `g_castLock`). Each source publishes a `HandView { kind (Heal / Offense / Buff / Ward / ConcBuff / Idle), spell, target, concentration, chargeFrac, firesIn }`, and EACH source reads the OTHER's HandView when it picks. "Knows what is on the other one at all times" = one shared read function `OtherHand(follower, hand)` (one reader, principle 12), never two code paths guessing.

Independence rules (the Arch-Mage rule set; regular casters get the same mechanism with the weaker policy below):
- A pick is made PER HAND. The pair is legal when the two spells do not contradict (see "pairing table").
- Simultaneous fire is allowed, never required. A concentration in one hand (Stride-type buff, ward, flames) is a long-lived tenant: the other hand's picks run around it.
- Dual-cast stays a separate mode (both hands one spell, perk-gated); it takes both sources and is picked ahead of the pair.

## What must change in today's code
1. THE ACTOR-WIDE CAST LATCH (the "cast latch is actor-wide and hand-blind" finding; memory `cast-latch-is-actor-wide-hand-blind`): the engine/AI latch that "equipped but never cast" came from, and MFO's own `g_fired` / `g_fireSeen` / `ObservedFiring` / the self-stream registry, treat a cast as the actor's, not a hand's. The per-hand fire evidence already exists (`HandFireSince`, the SpellFire graph events `MLh_`/`MRh_`, `ComposedCast::LastFireHand`); the carrier must key EVERY "did it fire / is it in flight / may I cast now" read by (follower, hand). The unkeyed ones (NoteObservedCast's `hand = 0` when both hands watch the same form; the direct roads' actor-wide cooldown, `Loadout::CoolingDown`) are the work list. A cooldown that is per actor stops the second hand for no reason.
2. HAND CLAIMS: heal already has one claim slot per hand. Offense/buff/ward claims are one "LEFT" claim today (`ClaimOffenseCast` hand LEFT; BuffClaim LEFT; the IDLE-HAND FLOOR claims the other hand deny-only so no un-gambited spell arms there). For two sources the floor becomes "a hand with no claim of ours is denied; a hand with a claim is driven", and the offense/buff claims take a hand argument chosen by the pairing logic. Harbinger side: a second simultaneous kIntent_Cast claim per actor must be served (the 11-17 right-hand offense + left heal shows APMF already holds one per hand for the heal+offense pair; whether two OFFENSE or offense+conc-buff claims coexist is a Harbinger ask to verify in the field before building).
3. IAUS ONE PICK PER LAP: the engine returns one action per follower per lap. The carrier needs one lap to produce up to TWO casting picks, one per source, scored against the same book: (a) pick the best spell for the free hand given the other hand's HandView; (b) pick for the other hand only if idle or its tenant is dispensable. Smallest change: run the cast considerations twice per lap with a per-hand context (`hand`, `otherHand`), the second pass seeing the first pass's pick as the other hand's HandView. The posture / movement picks stay single.
4. hand_lost cost (`feat/conc-hand-cost`): today a concentration self-buff/ward costs `hand_lost` when the follower's fighting weapon needs the channelling hand. With two sources it generalises to "the opportunity cost of parking a tenant on this hand = what the OTHER-hand decisions lose": for a caster with spells in both hands, parking a concentration Stride in L costs L's future offense options, not weapon damage; for the Arch-Mage that cost is small (the other hand still attacks), for a regular caster it is large. The cost keeps its shape (top-foe share of forgone value, placeholder curve, never a veto) and gains a second term fed by `OtherHand`. Its input switches from "weapon hand" to "which source is free".
5. THE CAST-GAMBIT LOCK (`g_castLock.hand[h]`, the "cast gambit locked -- spell X still firing (left hand)" rule): already per hand, with rank carried. It must stay per hand and stop blocking the OTHER hand: today `HELD OFF -- spell X wants the follower's left hand, spell Y is still firing there` lines are right (same hand), and a pick for the other hand must not be starved by it. The Arch-Mage carrier needs two additions: (a) a lock names (hand, spell, kind, concentration) so a concentration tenant is a long lock the other hand never contests, (b) preemption (`PreemptHand`, `CanPreemptHand`) compares ranks within a hand only. Rank order across hands stays gambit order; IAUS rows carry a synthetic rank as now.
6. DENY COMPLETENESS (principle 2): a hand we drive must be denied to the AI's own picks, a hand we hold must not let the engine's AI cast beside our claim (the `OTHER FIRED` line, now logged by MFO in `NoteObservedCast`, is the detector: 10:36:55 Incinerate beside our Shadow Drain claim). Two sources means two driven hands; the AI-owned leftovers (casters nobody claims) are the holes to close.

## Pairing table (what is legal in L + R)
| L \ R | Offense | Heal | Ward / conc buff | Summon | Same spell |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Offense | yes (two different foes or one foe) | yes | yes | yes | dual-cast mode only |
| Heal | yes | different recipient only (already built) | yes | yes | dual-cast heal (built for archers) |
| Ward / conc buff | yes (the Stride + attack case) | yes | one conc tenant per effect family, no two wards | yes | no |
| Summon | yes | yes | yes | one per fire | no |
Contradictions to refuse: two concentrations whose effects overlap (same active-effect archetype), a heal and a stagger the heal's recipient would be hit by, anything that needs the same hand twice. Magicka is shared: the reserve floor and `affordable` consider the SUM of the live concentration drains plus the new cost.

## Arch-Mage versus a regular caster
Same two sources, same `OtherHand` reader, different POLICY table (principle 12: one named tuning table, not two code paths):
- Arch-Mage unique: plans the pair. Scores the pair jointly (pairing bonus for a ward/Stride + attack, attack + attack on two foes, heal + attack), may hold a concentration tenant while the other hand fires many casts, switches the free hand's role per lap, fires both at once when the pair scores best. Reads firesIn / chargeFrac of the other hand to stagger (a heal timed into the other hand's recovery).
- Regular caster (marth: "significantly less intelligently"): the second source is opportunistic only. It fills the idle hand with a pick that does not conflict, never plans a pair, never parks a concentration for the pair's sake, and falls back to one source the moment magicka is below the reserve. Implemented as the same loop with the joint-pair term weighted ~0 and `maxTenants = 1`.
The class table (`classes/ClassTable.cpp`, the carrier slot named in the log line) owns the policy values; the carrier is the code that reads them.

## Hard rules the build inherits
- Every cast in either source takes an ANIMATED road (animations required for ALL actions). `self-stream` / direct-force never carry one of the two sources. Frost Nova-type self AoE needs its animated claim road first (backlog item); that is a prerequisite, not a part of this.
- No co-save change is implied (hand views are runtime). Threading: sources are read on the worker (#83-#85), hand state is from plain loads like `CastInFlightOnHand`.
- Field proof order (principle 5): first observe passively that two claims (offense + conc buff, offense + offense) coexist and both fire under Harbinger, using the 11-17 shape as the existing proof for heal + offense; only then build the pairing policy.
- Harbinger asks: two-offense / offense + conc-buff claim coexistence per actor; per-hand deny of AI picks while a claim drives the other hand; the claim-repoint case behind `OTHER FIRED 0002B96C` (10:28:52): a repointed claim whose old spell still fires.
